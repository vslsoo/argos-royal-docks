Canning Town and Pontoon Dock: the largest, fastest-growing part of the Royal Docks, with no Argos store within walking distance

Both areas lie inside the GLA Opportunity Area and are growing three times faster than Newham as a whole. No Argos store is within a 20-minute walk; only collection points reach into Canning Town.

Argos

No Argos store, standalone or inside Sainsbury's, is within a 20-minute walk of either area. Two collection points across the River Lea, Leven Road and Bromley-by-Bow, cover about half of Canning Town in 20 minutes, but they hold no stock and only hand over online orders. Pontoon Dock has no Argos point of any kind within 20 minutes.
