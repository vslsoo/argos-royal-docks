Canning Town and Pontoon Dock: the most populous parts of the growth area, with no Argos nearby

The two selected MSOAs had 29,215 residents in mid-2024 (+21.0% since 2021; Newham overall +6.6%), with ≈ 21,500 more expected from approved and under-construction housing schemes. Both sit inside the Royal Docks and Beckton Riverside Opportunity Area, and neither is within a 20-minute walk of an Argos.

Argos

No part of either area lies within a 20-minute walk of any Argos. The nearest stores on the north bank are Stratford Broadway (2.0 km from Canning Town) and the Argos in Sainsbury's Beckton (2.4 km from Pontoon Dock). South of the river, the Woolwich store's walking zone reaches only the edge of North Woolwich, through the Woolwich Foot Tunnel.

Sources: stores — OpenStreetMap (shop=catalogue), not yet checked against argos.co.uk; stores inside Sainsbury's identified by location; walking isochrones — Valhalla routing on OpenStreetMap data; distances are straight-line to the MSOA boundary.
