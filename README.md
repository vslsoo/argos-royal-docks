# Argos · Royal Docks

Interactive atlas for a Huff-model location study of a new Argos in Royal Docks, Newham (London).

**Live site:** https://vslsoo.github.io/argos-royal-docks/

## Sections
- **Study area** — why Canning Town (MSOA Newham 034) and Pontoon Dock (MSOA Newham 042) form the demand zone: population and growth, the GLA Opportunity Area, walking catchments of the 10 nearest Argos points (stores, Argos in Sainsbury's, collection points), and planned housing growth.
- **Competitors** — close-up of the study area and a 15-minute walk around it: every shop in OpenStreetMap, classified by how much it overlaps the Argos range. No direct competitor and no general-merchandise chain; 24 shops overlap a single category.

## Data sources
- Population: ONS mid-year estimates 2021 and 2024 by LSOA (Nomis NM_2014_1); households: ONS Census 2021, TS041
- Boundaries: ONS LSOA 2021 (BFE/BFC); MSOA names: House of Commons Library
- Opportunity Area boundary and OAPF figures: GLA, Royal Docks and Beckton Riverside OAPF (2023)
- Housing schemes: GLA Planning London Datahub
- Argos stores and collection points: Argos store locator (argos.co.uk/stores, 9 Nov 2026)
- Shops: OpenStreetMap via Overpass API (9 Nov 2026); Woolwich Foot Tunnel: OpenStreetMap; walking isochrones: Valhalla (OSM)
- Basemap: Esri World Light Gray Canvas
